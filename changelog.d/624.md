### Changed

- core-hooks (Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity 1.3.0): `zsh_traps.py` denies each unquoted `=word` that zsh expands, also of a command (`echo =ls`), in array elements, `[[ ]]` operands, default words, assignment parts after `:`, brace expansion, and words after empty parts (`""==y`). It passes forms that zsh does not expand, such as `<<=EOF` and `time x==y`. It denies a command that it cannot read, such as substitutions nested deeper than it reads (#627, #628). See [#624](https://github.com/aeghnnsw/cc-toolkit/issues/624).
