### Changed

- dev-skills (Claude Code 3.88.0, Codex 1.74.0, Antigravity 1.43.0): in t3-project-lead, `task verified` refuses a running task with no recorded PR unless the lead gives `--no-pr`, and names `task pr` and `task merged`. `task worker` settles each task whose thread it takes, and `wait --settles` gives a refused settle to the user, so neither settle has a due line. Lead action 7 names the triage label in the brief of the worker that files it. Workers ask for all approvals of a task in one numbered message (#633, #634, #635). See [#631](https://github.com/aeghnnsw/cc-toolkit/issues/631).
- Repository: ADR 0008 records the `--no-pr`, `task worker`, and `wait --settles` rules, the t3-project-lead tests and scenarios cover them and the approvals sentence, and the step word limit is 280 words (#633, #635). See [#631](https://github.com/aeghnnsw/cc-toolkit/issues/631).
