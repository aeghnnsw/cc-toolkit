### Changed

- dev-skills (Claude Code 3.78.1, Antigravity 1.33.1): In pressure-test, `codex_critic.py wait` reports `running`, not `error:no call`, while a start holds `start.lock` and its supervisor has not yet written `call.json`. Before, a `wait` after a stopped or busy start could report `error:no call` in that gap. See [#538](https://github.com/aeghnnsw/cc-toolkit/issues/538).
