### Changed

- core-hooks (Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity 1.3.1): `zsh_traps.py` denies an unquoted `=word` in a command substitution of a heredoc body, such as `$(echo ==q9)` in the body of `cat <<EOF`. zsh runs these substitutions when the end word has no quote or backslash, also inside quote characters, `${...}`, and arithmetic. A body with a quoted end word, such as `<<'EOF'`, and body text outside a substitution give no finding. A body ends where zsh ends it: at a line that is exactly the end word, after the leading tabs for `<<-`. See [#636](https://github.com/aeghnnsw/cc-toolkit/issues/636).
