### Changed

- core-hooks (Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity 1.3.2): in Claude Code and Codex, `zsh_traps.py` runs the corrected command in place of a command whose only trap is a separator word that names no command, such as `for n in 1 2; do gh issue view $n; echo ======; done`, with no denial and no notice. A command with `${`, `$'`, a backslash-newline, or `eval`, or with another trap, keeps its denial. Antigravity keeps its block. See [#670](https://github.com/aeghnnsw/cc-toolkit/issues/670).
